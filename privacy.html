Last updated: September 3, 2026

AK Studio ("we", "us", "our") builds and maintains websites for businesses. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit theakstudio.in or get in touch with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

How we use your information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

This site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to collect anonymized, aggregated usage statistics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings or opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

When we build or manage a project for you, delivery may involve third-party services you choose or approve — for example, domain registrars, hosting providers, or payment gateways. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Data retention

We retain client and enquiry information only for as long as needed to deliver the service, meet legal or accounting obligations, or as agreed with you. You can request that we delete your data at any time by contacting us.

Your rights
